A temp knight is a helper that a knight makes for one task and clears when the task is done.
It is a real agent, with its own prompt, picture and conversation, so you can open its room and talk to it.
It is not a member of the team: it takes no seat in a group and no seat at the round table.

## Ask for helpers

You do not make one yourself.
Ask a knight for work that splits into parts that can run on their own:

```text
Compare these three papers. Give each paper to its own helper, then merge what they find.
```

The knight calls `helper_create` once per part, and ends its turn.
Each temp knight starts at once.
Its answer comes back to the knight's conversation as a new turn, the way a `message_agent` answer does.
When the answers are in, the knight merges them and calls `helper_end` to clear the helpers that are done.

| Field of `helper_create` | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `display_name` | The name shown on its messages, 1 to 32 characters. |
| `prompt` | Who the helper is. |
| `task` | The task, self-contained: what to do, what to read, and what to answer with. |
| `name` | Optional. The permanent name. Without one, it is the knight's name and the next free number, such as `researcher-1`. |
| `class`, `color` | Optional. Its look. |
| `model`, `thinking` | Optional. They default to the knight's own. |
| `skills` | Optional. Skills from `skill_list`, while the skills addon is on. |

A temp knight has no agent, group, archive, channel-arrange or schedule tools, and it cannot make temp knights of its own.
Both tools are on the `member` tier.

## Limits and cleanup

- A knight has at most 4 temp knights at once, and the host at most 12.
  At a limit, the knight's oldest finished temp knight is archived to make room.
  The call is refused only when every one of them is busy.
- A temp knight is *finished* when nothing runs for it, nothing is queued, no card waits for you and no message from its knight is unanswered.
  The knight does not have to say so.
- A finished temp knight is archived by itself after 24 hours.
- An archived temp knight keeps its history.
  Restore it from the archive page, or with **Undo** in the notice that follows a clearing.

Change the numbers, or turn the tools off, with the `helpers` setting:

```ts
defineRoundtable({
	helpers: { perParent: 4, perHost: 12, archiveFinishedAfterHours: 24 },
	// helpers: false,  // offers neither helper_create nor helper_end
});
```

`perParent` is 1 to 8, `perHost` 1 to 50 and `archiveFinishedAfterHours` 1 to 720, or `false` to keep them until you clear them.
A value out of range stops the start, and so does a `perParent` above `perHost`.

## Where they live

On Discord, a temp knight is a thread under its knight's channel.
Its messages show under its own name and picture.
Archiving it archives and locks the thread, and restoring it opens the thread again.
In the web app, a temp knight is a room, listed under its knight.

In the owner app, the temp knights hang under their knight in the room list, each with a small face and a state: working, waiting for you, or ended.
**Clear finished temp knights** is in the room list's menu, in a folder's menu and in a knight's menu.
It archives them at once, and the notice offers **Undo**.
A temp knight's own menu has **Archive this temp knight**.
When a knight clears its helpers itself, its room shows a line that names them, with an **Undo** too.
