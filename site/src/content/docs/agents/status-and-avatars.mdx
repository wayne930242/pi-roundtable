## The status message

The bot keeps a `#dashboard` channel at the top of your server, outside any category.
It holds one message from the bot, and the message updates itself.

<Image src={agentStatus} alt="A Discord message listing three agents, Guide, Researcher and Editor, with their channels, models and whether each is idle or working." />

The bot finds the message again after a restart and makes a new one if you delete it.
It pins the message when the bot has the Pin Messages permission; without it the message still works, and the bot logs a warning once.

### What it shows

Under the title there is an "Updated" time, in your configured time zone, and any lines that plugins add.
The words on the message follow `locale`.
The examples below use English; `locale: "zh-TW"` makes the bot write them in Chinese.

Each active agent has a block with:

- Its display name and a link to its channel.
- Its model and thinking level.
- Its state: `Idle`, `Working` with the channel it works in, or a count of queued messages.
  A queue is shown next to Working, as in "2 queued".
- How much of its context window is used, such as `context 12k / 200k (6%)`.
  Right after the context is compacted it says so, and the figure returns at the next turn.
- How many schedules it has, and when it was last active, or "No activity since startup".

Each active group has its display name, a link to its channel, its members and host, whether it is idle or has replies in progress, and its last activity.

When a list is too long for one Discord message, the block ends with a count of the entries that were left out.
Archived agents and groups are not listed.

### When it refreshes

The message updates when something changes, such as a turn starting or ending or an agent being created.
Discord limits message edits, so the bot edits it at most once every 10 seconds.
It also refreshes every 5 minutes, so the last-activity times and the context figures stay current.

## Avatars

Each agent speaks in Discord under its own name and picture.
Each picture is the agent's character, composed from parts and drawn by the bot itself: no image provider is involved.

- A new agent gets the look its role suggests: a class (wizard, knight, scholar, ranger and 20 more) in one of eight colors.
  `agent_create` accepts a `class` and a `color` to choose them.
- The `agent_avatar` tool changes the look later: a class, a color, or single parts such as the hat, prop, hair, build, or skin.
  What it leaves out stays as it is, and a new class keeps the agent's colors.
- The owner's profile panel (`/profile`) has a class dropdown and a color dropdown that do the same.
- The new picture shows from the agent's next message.
- Every agent present before 0.23 gets a character at its first start, so its Discord picture changes once.

The bot needs `canvas` with SVG support (librsvg) to draw the pictures, and refuses to start the agent server without it.
`bunx roundtable doctor` runs the `characters` check for this.

<Aside type="note" title="Drawn avatars are deprecated">
Drawing agents' pictures with an `images` provider, the `avatar` setting, and `avatarPrompt` are deprecated and ignored; they are removed in 1.0.
The `images` slot still powers `generate_image`.
</Aside>

### Where the pictures are served

The bot stores avatars in the data directory and serves them at `PUBLIC_URL`, under `/avatars/`.
Discord fetches them from there, so the address has to reach the process from the internet.
