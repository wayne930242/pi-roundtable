Two settings in `roundtable.config.ts` decide how the bot speaks and what time it means:

```ts
export default {
	// ...
	locale: "zh-TW",
	timeZone: "Europe/Berlin",
} satisfies RoundtableConfig;
```

| Key | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `locale` | `"en"` or `"zh-TW"` | `"en"` |
| `timeZone` | An IANA time zone name, such as `Europe/Berlin` or `Asia/Taipei` | `"UTC"` |

The host applies both once, when it starts, and they hold for the whole process.
Change them, then restart the bot.

## `locale`

`locale` chooses the message catalog for the text the bot shows in Discord.
Both catalogs have the same keys.
Among other things it covers:

- The approval and question cards, with their buttons and footers.
- The stop button and the note on a long turn.
- The `/roundtable` command's description, its refusal for non-owners, and the schedule panel.
- The self-updating status message and the notices a conversation posts, such as a failed reply or a stopped turn.
- How a time zone is named in tool descriptions: the English catalog uses the IANA name (`Europe/Berlin`), and the `zh-TW` catalog uses the place name (`Berlin`).

A value other than `en` or `zh-TW` is an error:

```text
config locale: expected a locale, en or zh-TW, got "<value>". Fix the value in roundtable.config.ts.
```

What `locale` does not change:

- The audit-log reasons and the refusals of the Discord administration tools read as English in both locales.
- The text the core adds to the agents' prompts is English.
- Your prompts, your agents, and your plugins' text are yours to write in any language.

## `timeZone`

`timeZone` is the zone the bot uses for anything with a clock time.

- A schedule's one-off time (`"YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"`) and its repeating time of day (`"HH:MM"`) are in this zone, and the schedule tools say so in their descriptions.
- Time stamps in schedule messages and lists, and in the output of `channel_read`, use it.
- "Today", for the upcoming events that go into each speaker's memory block, is the date in this zone.

The name is checked when the bot starts, not by the configuration check.
`roundtable doctor` accepts any non-empty string.
A zone the runtime does not recognise stops `roundtable start` once the checks have passed:

```text
Invalid time zone: <value>
```

For schedules in practice, see [Schedules and background work](/agents/schedules-and-background/).
