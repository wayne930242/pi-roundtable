A turn the shutdown cuts short no longer loses its message: a turn still running at the drain limit that has not answered by the end of the abort grace (stopped, failed, or still running) has each person's message it has not answered, the one that started it and those steered into it, refused as a waiting message is, before the messages waiting behind turns and the teardown, within `refuseWaitingSeconds`. With `discord.catchUp` an addressed one is recorded as left, its channel is told once that it will be answered after the restart, and the next process answers it once; elsewhere its channel is told once to send it again. A message is answered once a turn run through `settleTurn` (`context.turns`, the agent team) in the work of its admission, including tasks it queues, ends `ok`; a background turn, another agent's `message_agent` turn and a new agent's opening task do not count. An agent message the cut turn's chain sent, at any hop, is taken out of the outbox again when its person's message is refused, since the turn runs again. A turn or queue task of the admission that the stopping host refuses (asked for during the drain) refuses its unanswered messages too, and the runtime's refusal posts the stopped notice rather than a failure that asks to try again. A turn under a refused message that ends later posts the stopped notice instead of its answer, so the message is not answered twice. What the cut turn did before it stopped is not undone: a write held for approval is held again in the next process's turn, but a call that ran without approval may run again.

At the drain limit of a shutdown, the host stops the running turn of a channel whose claim has no `stop`, such as a plugin's claim that runs its turns through `context.turns`, with the runtime's `stop(channel)`, so the turn ends within the abort grace instead of running on until the process exits. A claim's own `stop` is used as before, and the Stop button and `conversations.stop` are unchanged.
