A turn the shutdown cuts short no longer loses its message: a turn still running at the drain limit that has not answered by the end of the abort grace (stopped, failed, or still running) has each person's message it was answering, the one that started it and those steered into it, refused as a waiting message is, before the messages waiting behind turns and the teardown, within `refuseWaitingSeconds`. With `discord.catchUp` an addressed one is recorded as left, its channel is told once that it will be answered after the restart, and the next process answers it once; elsewhere its channel is told once to send it again. It answered when a turn run under that message through `settleTurn` (`context.turns`, the agent team) ended `ok`; a turn under a message left this way that ends later posts the stopped notice instead of its answer, so the message is not answered twice. What the cut turn did before it stopped is not undone: a write held for approval is held again in the next process's turn, but a call that ran without approval may run again.

At the drain limit of a shutdown, the host stops the running turn of a channel whose claim has no `stop`, such as a plugin's claim that runs its turns through `context.turns`, with the runtime's `stop(channel)`, so the turn ends within the abort grace instead of running on until the process exits. A claim's own `stop` is used as before, and the Stop button and `conversations.stop` are unchanged.
