Diagrams in a document page draw with the page's own colours in the light and dark schemes, so label boxes match the page and labels keep their contrast, and their padding is tighter.

A diagram carries alt text, a title and a description that name its type, its node count and its first edges, in English or Traditional Chinese, for screen readers.

A diagram much wider than its panel keeps its own size and the panel scrolls sideways instead of shrinking it to unreadable text.

Updates pi-better-show from 0.5.0 to 0.8.0.
